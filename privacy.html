Privacy & analytics

This personal portfolio is maintained by Alex Tsvetanov. Contact me about privacy.

Optional analytics

When Google Analytics is configured, it loads only after you choose “Allow analytics”. It helps measure page visits, CV downloads, email-link clicks and visits to external websites. Declining leaves the portfolio fully usable. No advertising features are enabled.

Page addresses sent by this site exclude query strings and fragments. Email addresses and the full destination of external links are not included in custom events. Google receives technical connection and browser information when analytics is enabled. See Google’s privacy policy.

Your analytics choice is saved on this device for up to 180 days. You can change it below when analytics is configured. Withdrawing consent stops tracking and removes this site’s Google Analytics cookies.

Other local storage and services

The merchandise preview saves your selected designs in your browser. It does not submit an order or collect payment. Some portfolio designs load fonts, icons and libraries from external providers. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process technical access information independently of optional analytics. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
